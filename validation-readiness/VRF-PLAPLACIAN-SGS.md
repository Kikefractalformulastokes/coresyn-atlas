## Exact claim
A two-dimensional LES/SGS benchmark package compares a p-Laplacian closure against the bare solver, Smagorinsky, dynamic Smagorinsky and Leith closures. After a minimal archived Leith integration patch, the package passes its nine test programs and reproduces the recorded demo, figures and benchmark tables in the internal environment.

## Real state
The original artifact `plaplacian-sgs-2d-v1.0.0.tar` has been recovered. It includes source code, MIT licence, `CITATION.cff`, nine test programs, scripts, figures and raw result tables.

The original untouched TAR is not 9/9: it passes 8/9 and fails `test_leith.py` because the solver does not accept or apply the included Leith parameter/RHS. The archived `LEITH_INTEGRATION_FIX.patch` is minimal and resolves that integration defect. A patched copy passes 9/9.

The seed-42 demo and four figures reproduce the archived outputs. The dynamic-Smagorinsky benchmark, standard-Leith benchmark and optimized-Leith benchmark have been rerun against the patched artifact and reproduce the stored tables. No external reviewer has rerun the package.
