## Commercial thesis

Many organisations already calculate their carbon footprint internally, with public calculators, consultants or ESG teams. CoreSyn should not compete as another generic carbon calculator. CoreSyn sells independent evidence assurance, verification-readiness, reduction intelligence and project/funding preparation.

Core proposition:

"Ya has calculado tu huella. CoreSyn comprueba si puedes demostrarla."

CoreSyn does not claim to be an accredited verifier unless and until it holds the accreditation required for the applicable procedure. Where accredited verification is legally or procedurally required, CoreSyn positions as pre-verification / evidence assurance / verification readiness and can partner with accredited third parties.

## Customer pains

1. Excel exists but data provenance is weak.
2. Scope boundaries are unclear or inconsistent year-to-year.
3. Emission factors may be outdated, wrong-year or not applicable.
4. Supporting invoices / meter data / fuel records are incomplete.
5. Scope 3 is material but poorly evidenced.
6. Companies fear rejection, rework or audit questions.
7. SMEs cannot justify Big Four fees for a bounded review.
8. Sustainability teams spend too much time manually reconciling spreadsheets and evidence.
9. Companies do not know which reduction project to prioritise after measuring emissions.
10. Funding programmes require a coherent project, baseline, KPIs and evidence.

## Product ladder

### Carbon QuickScan
Price hypothesis: EUR 1,500–3,000
Scope: one footprint workbook + key evidence + top gaps.
Output: issue list, evidence-readiness score, major inconsistencies, recommended next step.
Sales objective: fast entry product.

### Carbon Evidence Gate
Price hypothesis: EUR 5,000–15,000
Scope: full reconstruction of selected footprint boundaries.
Output: evidence map, provenance, factor checks, recalculation, anomalies, missing evidence, PASS / CONDITIONAL / HOLD / FAIL / NOT TESTED by evidence block.

### Verification Readiness Pack
Price hypothesis: EUR 8,000–20,000
Scope: Carbon Evidence Gate plus dossier structured for external/accredited verification where required.
Output: verifier-ready evidence index, unresolved issues, assumptions register, scope statement, data lineage.

### Carbon Reduction & Decarbonisation Diagnostic
Price hypothesis: EUR 10,000–30,000
Scope: verified/reconstructed baseline + reduction opportunities + prioritisation + KPI framework.
Output: reduction roadmap, abatement candidates, evidence requirements, decision memo.

### Carbon Project Intelligence & Funding Pack
Price hypothesis: EUR 20,000–75,000
Scope: one decarbonisation project from opportunity to funding readiness.
Output: project case, baseline, expected reduction methodology, ODS/impact alignment where relevant, partners, funding routes, evidence pack.

### Enterprise Carbon Evidence Infrastructure
Price hypothesis: EUR 75,000–250,000+ annually
Scope: multi-site / multi-country / recurring evidence infrastructure.
Output: automated ingestion, evidence graph, year-on-year control, exception management, audit trail, reporting support.
