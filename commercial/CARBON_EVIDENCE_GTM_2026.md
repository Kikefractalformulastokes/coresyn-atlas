## 1. Market reality — 2026

### What is actually mandatory

Real Decreto 214/2025 obliges companies already subject to non-financial-information duties to calculate their organisational carbon footprint annually, prepare a quantified reduction plan of at least five years, and make the information public. It does NOT make MITECO registration mandatory for all private companies.

For the MITECO voluntary registry, minimum footprint scope is Scope 1 + 2. MITECO states that Scope 3 and Scope 1+2 for non-SMEs or SMEs with process emissions must be independently verified. The MITECO calculator is voluntary; for certain SMEs calculating only Scope 1+2, use of the official calculator can exempt the need to present an accredited verification certificate for registry purposes.

The MITECO open-data portal publishes a downloadable list of registered organisational carbon footprints, as well as compensation movements and absorption projects. This is a legal public lead-intelligence source.

### ETS / industrial market

The 2026–2030 EU ETS free-allocation cycle affects 664 fixed industrial installations in Spain, with an average 38.1 million free allowances/year and an estimated annual value of roughly EUR 3 billion at the market price cited by MITECO on 26 Aug 2026. Annual activity-level reports are due by 28 February for installations with free allocation. This market uses formal MRVA requirements, approved monitoring methodology plans, and accredited verification.

### Market structure

Spain had 3,310,824 economically active enterprises at 1 Jan 2025. 81.6% had two or fewer employees, so the entire SME universe is not commercially addressable for a paid carbon-assurance product. The practical market is the subset with external pressure: industrial supply chains, tenders, customer questionnaires, financing, ISO/EMAS systems, sustainability reporting, MITECO registration, or client-imposed Scope 3 demands.

High-priority sectors by combination of emission relevance, larger average company size and external pressure:
1. Industry
2. Transport and logistics
3. Construction / infrastructure
4. Food and hospitality groups
5. B2B technology / services exposed to enterprise procurement
6. Energy-intensive commercial operations

# Carbon Evidence Red Team

Independent pre-verification and evidence assurance over ANY existing footprint calculation.

- Dcycle export

- consultant workbook
- Big Four workbook
- ERP extract
- client's own calculation

CoreSyn questions:
1. Can every material number be traced to primary evidence?
2. Is the factor correct for year, geography, source and unit?
3. Are organisational and operational boundaries internally consistent?
4. Can the result be reproduced independently?
5. Are there missing or contradictory records?
6. Which conclusions are supported, conditional or unsupported?
7. What must be fixed before formal verification, tender, customer disclosure or registry submission?

Output:
PASS / CONDITIONAL / HOLD / FAIL / NOT TESTED by evidence block + exception register + data-lineage map + corrected/reconstructed calculation where possible.

This is deliberately orthogonal to the software vendor and complementary to the accredited verifier.
