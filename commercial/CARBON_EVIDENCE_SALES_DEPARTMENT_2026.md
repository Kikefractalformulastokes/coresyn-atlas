## Differentiators
1. Tool-neutral: accepts MITECO Excel, consultant workbook, ERP or carbon-software exports.
2. Evidence-first: every material result linked to provenance/evidence.
3. Adversarial review: actively looks for wrong factor/year/geography/unit, missing evidence, double counting, boundary inconsistency and non-reproducible results.
4. Explicit epistemic states: PASS / CONDITIONAL / HOLD / FAIL / NOT TESTED.
5. No forced software migration.
6. Human independent QA; AI assists extraction/reconciliation but does not issue autonomous legal certification.
7. Complementary to accredited verifiers.
8. Fixed-scope SME entry product with escalation to Scope 3, verification readiness, reduction/funding and recurring control.
