## 25-minute discovery structure

0–3 min: context and why this conversation
3–10 min: current project/problem, urgency, stakeholders
10–16 min: evidence, models, data, KPIs and gaps
16–20 min: funding / regulatory / investment context
20–23 min: propose one bounded CoreSyn diagnostic or Gate
23–25 min: agree next action, owner and date
