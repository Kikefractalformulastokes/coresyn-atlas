## Funnel stages and definitions

SIGNAL
External trigger only: project, regulation, funding call, climate investment, sustainability programme.

QUALIFIED ACCOUNT
Evidence that CoreSyn has a plausible problem/offer fit.

VERIFIED CONTACT
Real, evidence-backed route; no guessed addresses.

OUTREACH SENT
Message sent successfully; no assumption of receipt by decision-maker.

HUMAN RESPONSE
A human answered. May be neutral, referral, rejection or interest.

QUALIFIED INTEREST
Human explicitly asks for details, product, proposal, call, pricing, capability evidence or internal referral to a relevant buyer.

MEETING BOOKED
Calendar event or explicit confirmed date/time.

DISCOVERY COMPLETE
Problem, buyer, urgency, scope, budget path and next decision understood.

PAID DIAGNOSTIC
Commercially agreed and invoiced/paid entry engagement.

EVIDENCE GATE
Substantive assurance engagement.

EXPANSION
Additional site/category/project/portfolio scope.

ENTERPRISE / PORTFOLIO
Multi-site, multi-project or continuous infrastructure contract.
