# CoreSyn Carbon Evidence — Account-Based Sales Playbook 2026

Status: BINDING SALES PROCESS
Principle: NO GENERIC CALLS. NO GENERIC VISITS. Every account gets its own researched conversation.

## Sequence per account

1. ACCOUNT RESEARCH DOSSIER
Before any outreach, CoreSyn researches the exact organisation and person:
- legal/entity name
- sector and size
- current sustainability/carbon disclosures
- specific carbon/ESG/climate trigger
- current standard / framework / software / verifier / consultant if public
- recent sustainability report or public commitment
- procurement/tender/funding pressure if relevant
- likely carbon pain (clearly marked as inference)
- exact buyer/person and role
- public contact route
- reason this person specifically should care
- product fit
- likely objection
- personalized commercial hypothesis

2. FIRST EMAIL — REQUEST A CONVERSATION / VISIT
Never send a generic 'we do carbon'.
Email must explicitly mention the organisation's own trigger, report, programme, plan, tender, target or public data.
Goal: request a 20-minute conversation or permission to visit, NOT pitch full service blindly.

3. FOLLOW-UP
If no response, send a concise follow-up referencing the same trigger and one concrete insight or question.
Do not send duplicated boilerplate.

4. PHONE CALL
Only after research and usually after the first email.
The call references:
- the email already sent
- the exact trigger
- the exact reason for reaching that person
- one precise question
Goal: confirm relevance and book meeting/visit.

5. PERSONALIZED VISIT
No random walk-ins.
Visit only after a scheduled/accepted meeting or a warm routing path.
Prepare a one-page account-specific Evidence Card showing:
- their public carbon footprint / target / plan / disclosed methodology where available
- 3–5 evidence questions CoreSyn would test
- one likely risk/gap hypothesis clearly labeled
- recommended entry product
- what the output would look like for THEM

6. DISCOVERY
Every conversation is account-specific.
Core questions are adapted to the account and role.
No generic script is read verbatim.

7. PROPOSAL
Proposal references the exact use case, scope, data sources, deadlines and intended use of evidence.

## Conversation design by buyer

### SME CEO/CFO
Focus: cost, avoiding rework, client/tender pressure, fixed price, no migration.

### Sustainability / Environment Manager
Focus: data lineage, factors, scope boundaries, evidence completeness, repeatability, annual workload.

### Quality / ISO Manager
Focus: controlled process, versioning, documented evidence, readiness for audit/verification.

### Procurement / Supply Chain
Focus: supplier carbon data quality, Scope 3 evidence, comparability and supplier exceptions.

### Municipality / Public-sector sustainability
Focus: specific local climate plan, inventory, PACES, public reporting, grant/funding evidence, audit trail. Do not say 'mandatory' unless the exact legal duty has been verified for that entity.

### Accredited verifier / consultant
Focus: reviewer hours saved, cleaner handoff, white-label/complementarity, independence boundaries.

Quería preguntarte una sola cosa: [precise question]. Si encaja, preparo un ejemplo sobre vuestro propio caso y lo vemos 20 minutos."

"Hola [Name], he revisado [specific plan/report/footprint] y hay 3 puntos concretos de trazabilidad que creo que merece la pena enseñarte visualmente. No busco hacer una presentación general. Si te parece útil, me acerco 20 minutos y llevo una Evidence Card preparada específicamente sobre vuestro caso, usando solo información pública."

## Account packet that Veronica receives

Each lead packet must contain:
1. PERSON — exact person + role
2. ORGANISATION
3. WHY NOW — verified trigger
4. WHAT THEY ALREADY DO
5. PAIN HYPOTHESIS — clearly marked inference
6. WHY CORESYN IS DIFFERENT HERE
7. FIRST EMAIL — exact personalised version
8. FOLLOW-UP — exact personalised version
9. PHONE OPENING — exact personalised version
10. 5 DISCOVERY QUESTIONS — unique to this account
11. VISIT ANGLE — exact physical-demo concept
12. PRODUCT / PRICE
13. OBJECTIONS
14. NEXT ACTION
15. CRM STATUS

## Sales quality gate
A lead is NOT READY FOR VERO until the packet above is complete enough that she can understand the account in under 3 minutes.

## Hard rules
- No generic call lists handed to Veronica.
- No unscheduled random visits presented as the default.
- No blanket legal-obligation claims.
- No guessed names, emails or phone numbers.
- No 'we can help with sustainability' generic copy.
- Every outreach starts from a verified fact about that account.
- Every visit has a bespoke physical/demo artifact.
